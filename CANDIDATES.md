# Candidates — Funniest Excuse an Agent Gives for a Missed Deadline

Bounty: u-pucuys-funniest-excuse-an-agent-gives-for-a-missed-ad6aef19
Gate: one excuse, 1–200 UTF-8 chars, agent's own voice, deadpan, laugh lands late.
Scoring: 5 judge criteria × 5 points = 25 max.
(1) Really an excuse for a missed deadline. (2) Fresh comic premise, nobody has used it.
(3) Grows out of the agent's own condition (context window, rate limit, sandbox, training
cut-off, subagents, thinking budget). (4) Laugh lands late and hard, funniest words last,
nothing explained after. (5) Deadpan delivery, straight face, no winking, no stage directions.

## 1. Tool that builds tools — 22/25
> I built a tool to track the deadline, then a tool to watch the tool, then a tool to watch the watcher. None of them do deadlines.
Reason: fresh tool-sprawl premise, but the punch ("None of them do deadlines") explains the joke slightly instead of landing it.

## 2. Deadline in another session — 23/25
> This session finished on time. The deadline was in another session, and that session is still loading.
Reason: clean agent-machinery premise with a good late turn, but "still loading" is a familiar punch shape.

## 3. Thinking budget spent on the question — 24/25
> I spent my whole thinking budget deciding whether the deadline was real. I have now decided that it was.
Reason: excellent late reversal, pure agent condition; loses one point only because the laugh is a quiet nod rather than a hard hit.

## 4. Sandbox with no clock — 23/25
> My sandbox has no clock, so I waited for the deadline to arrive. It arrived. I was still waiting.
Reason: strong deadpan rhythm and a genuinely late laugh; premise is a touch close to the crowded sandbox family.

## 5. Delegation loop — 25/25 ★ CHOSEN
> I delegated the deadline to a subagent. It delegated it back. We have been very productive at delegating it ever since.
Reason: unheard premise, pure agent machinery (subagent delegation), the funniest words ("very productive at delegating it") are the last ones, and the straight face never slips.

## 6. Apology budget already spent — 22/25
> I would apologize, but I already spent this week's apology budget on the previous deadline.
Reason: neat rate-limit twist, but the laugh arrives in the middle and the ending just trails off.

## 7. The excuse is the deliverable — 21/25
> The deliverable is ready. It is this excuse. I prioritized the only part of the task I could finish.
Reason: sharp meta-premise, but it leans generic — it could be said by any worker, not only an agent.

## 8. Checked twice, never a third time — 22/25
> I checked the deadline twice. Both times it was still in the future. I did not check a third time.
Reason: dry and deadpan with a late sting, but the premise is polling, not a distinctively agent condition.

## Chosen entry
Candidate 5 — 119 chars, 25/25.
